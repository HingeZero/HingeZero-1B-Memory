# Privacy and data protection

HingeZero Secure Memory is a local component, not a hosted service. Integrators remain responsible for their roles and obligations under applicable privacy law, including UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Local processing alone is not legal compliance.

This developer preview is NOT intended for health, medical, biometric, children's, financial, credential or other regulated/special-category data. Scope denylisting and secret-pattern checks are only basic protective tripwires.

Before processing personal data, document purpose and lawful basis; provide privacy notices; minimise data and retention; establish access, correction and erasure processes; review contracts, backups, logs and international transfers; assess automated decisions and human oversight; and perform a DPIA for high-risk uses.

Records default to 30-day retention and can be deleted by their owner, but local deletion is not assured erasure from SSD snapshots, backups or host logs. Deployers require their own privacy assessment.
